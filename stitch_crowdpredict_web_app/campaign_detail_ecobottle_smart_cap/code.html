Why EcoBottle Smart Cap?

Project Narrative

Standard reusable bottles harbor bacterial biofilms within 24 hours of use. Existing smart bottles force consumers to throw away their favorite insulated flasks for fragile, expensive all-in-one vessels. We engineered the EcoBottle Smart Cap as a universal drop-in lid replacement that transforms any standard wide-mouth bottle into a laboratory-grade water sanitation and intelligent hydration tracking station.

clean_hands

Real-Time UV Purification

Integrated deep UV-C LED arrays eradicate 99.999% of E.coli, Giardia, and common waterborne pathogens automatically every 2 hours, or instantly on a double-tap gesture.

monitoring

Autonomous Hydration Sync

High-precision capacitive depth sensors record continuous volumetric intake down to the milliliter, syncing seamlessly in background via Bluetooth 5.3 to iOS and Android wellness dashboards.

Designed and bench-tested over 14 months at our Lahore tech workspace, the cap incorporates food-grade 316 stainless steel interiors and BPA-free aerospace polymer housing. A single magnetic snap USB-C charge yields over 30 full days of active sanitization and hydration logging.
